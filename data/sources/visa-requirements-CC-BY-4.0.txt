UPSTREAM

The base matrix this dataset starts from is the open Passport Index data
(imorte/passport-index-data, https://github.com/imorte/passport-index-data),
published under the MIT licence. It is credited in `data/datapackage.json`
under `sources` and in the README. Every value that departs from it carries a
government source of its own; the values that do not depart from it are that
upstream's work, not ours, and we say so rather than absorbing it silently.
That is not politeness. A dataset whose stated purpose is that visa tables
circulate as unsourced copies of copies has no business being one.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS LICENCE DOES AND DOES NOT COVER

Individual facts - "a Portuguese passport holder may enter Japan without a
visa" - are not copyrightable anywhere we are aware of. The licence covers the
compilation: the selection, the normalisation into a single vocabulary, the
per-pair verification dates, and the change journal. If you take three facts
out of this repository you do not need our permission. If you take the table,
please credit it, because the credit is the only thing we get back for the
work of keeping it correct.
